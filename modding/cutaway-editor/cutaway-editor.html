Open your mod folder

Pick the mod's own folder (the one with level.json). The editor reads the cutaway pictures from its assets folder and saves assets/cutaway_anchors.json and the wheel sprites back into it.

Each vehicle skin needs assets/cutaway-<skin>.png (1536×1024, transparent background). A cutaway-<skin>-damaged.png drawn over the same outline is optional.

This browser cannot open folders. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

How to use

Drag any marker. Drag a seat's figure to move it, its lower-right corner to resize, the cyan dot to place the portrait disc, the red line for the cut rim in front of the seat (it hides the legs). Drag a wheel's centre, or its ring handle for the radius: line the ring up with the tyre outline, not the hub cap. With Spin preview on you see the blurred wheel exactly as the game draws it; a smeared rim means the ring is off.
