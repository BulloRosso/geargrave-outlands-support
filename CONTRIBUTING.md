# Contributing

Thank you for helping with Geargrave Outlands.

## What we accept

- **Translation files** created by the game's *Save for GitHub* button and
  uploaded to `i18n-contributions/<language>/`, one file per pull request. The
  automatic check refuses anything else.
- **Image defect issues** opened with the *Image defect* form. The easiest way
  is the game's **F10** reporter, which fills in the image ID for you.
- **Modding questions** opened with the *Modding question* form: questions about the
  mod format, validator mistakes, and features mods cannot do yet. Mods themselves
  are yours — share them wherever you like; this repository hosts the guide and the
  example, not a mod collection.

## Terms for contributions

By opening a pull request or an issue in this repository you confirm that:

1. the text you submit is your own work, or work you are allowed to submit;
2. you grant the developer of Geargrave Outlands a free, perpetual,
   worldwide, irrevocable and non-exclusive license to use, change and
   distribute your contribution as part of the game and its materials;
3. you do not expect payment. Contributors whose translations ship may be
   named in the game's credits under the name they entered in the game. If
   you would rather not be named, say so in your pull request.

## Guidelines for translations

- Keep placeholders such as `{0}` and `{1}` and markup such as `[b]…[/b]`.
  The game fills in names and numbers there.
- Keep the tone of the original. The game is dry, deadpan and a bit bleak, and
  a good translation sounds like it was written in your language.
- Keep translated names consistent with the rest of the game (factions,
  places, vehicles).
- Please stay friendly in the discussions. We review every contribution, and
  we may ask questions or suggest changes.
